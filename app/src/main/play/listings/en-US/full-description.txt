ReleaseKit Tasks is a minimal to-do list that keeps everything on your device.

• Add tasks in two taps
• Long-press a task to delete it
• Sort the list A–Z
• Share the list as plain text
• In-app updates: new versions install without leaving the app

Privacy first: your tasks are stored only on your phone. The app sends anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics (Firebase) so we can fix bugs faster. No ads, no account.
